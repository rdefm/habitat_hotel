# Spatial building cross-section UI + Bellhop Escort

## Problem Statement

Today's play surface is a stack of text-button lists (a Reception queue of party cards, a row of staffer/station cards, a room grid sorted alphabetically, a Terrace queue list) plus a bottom menu bar (Prices/Hire/Reports/Reviews) and a scrolling day-log ticker. A separate decorative strip (`ui/lobby_view.gd`) shows guests and staff walking between fixed "Entrance/Reception/Elevator" markers, but it's cosmetic only — it never reflects where a guest actually got seated, isn't a drag source or target, and sits awkwardly disconnected from the room grid below it. The player never sees their hotel as a place; they see menus.

## Solution

Replace the whole play surface below the stats strip with one unified, vertically-scrolling spatial view of the hotel as a building cross-section: Reception is the fixed ground floor where the arrival queue and Staff Pool live as draggable actors, the Terrace is its own fixed floor just above, and Room-type Floors stack above that in ascending unlock-star order. Guests and Staffers are actors, not cards — dragged from Reception's queue or the Staff Pool onto a Room/Station, and animating to their real target instead of a decorative stand-in. A seated guest's actor persists as the Room's idle occupant for their whole stay; a Staffer working a Job (cleaning, breakfast/dinner service) visibly travels to and stays at their real target. The bottom menu bar is retired — Prices/Hire/Reports/Reviews move behind tapping the Reception structure as one tabbed modal (mirroring the Terrace's existing tap-for-modal pattern from ADR-0010). The day log ticker is retired in favor of transient toasts anchored near the event's real spatial location.

Alongside this, ADR-0014's Bellhop Escort gets implemented for real: a staffed Bellhop now gives a Skill-scaled visible walk to the Room (replacing today's instant seat), with multiple assigned Bellhops each escorting one Party at a time in parallel and overflow Parties queuing for the next free Bellhop.

This is a rendering-layer amendment (ADR-0016) plus one Sim-layer addition (ADR-0017, implementing the already-accepted ADR-0014) — every other existing `Sim`/`GameState` call, and ADR-0001/0009's tap-and-drag interaction model, are unchanged.

## User Stories

1. As a player, I want to see my hotel as a real building — Reception at the bottom, the Terrace above it, Room floors stacked above that — so that the layout reads as a place instead of a form.
2. As a player, I want arriving Parties to visibly stand in a queue at Reception as individual characters, so that I can see who's waiting without opening a list.
3. As a player, I want to drag a waiting guest's actor onto an open Room to seat them, so that seating feels like placing someone rather than clicking a row in a menu.
4. As a player, I want tapping a waiting guest then tapping a Room to still work exactly as it does today, so that I'm not forced into drag-only interaction.
5. As a player, I want a seated guest's actor to visibly walk from Reception up to the actual Room I dropped them on, so that I get to watch the outcome of my choice instead of it happening off-screen.
6. As a player, I want a seated guest's actor to keep standing in their Room for the length of their stay, so that an occupied Room reads as occupied at a glance from across the whole building.
7. As a player, I want a Party that gets turned away to visibly walk back out from Reception, so that a lost booking is legible the same way a successful one is.
8. As a player, I want a checking-out guest's actor to walk back down from their Room through Reception and leave, so that checkout is the visible mirror of check-in.
9. As a player, I want to see every currently unassigned Staffer standing in a dedicated Staff Pool near Reception as a draggable character, so that I always know who's available to assign.
10. As a player, I want to drag a Staffer's actor onto a Station or an in-progress Job to (re)assign or Stack them, so that staffing feels like the same kind of direct placement as seating.
11. As a player, I want tapping a Staffer to still open their detail popup (Skill/Traits/current assignment) exactly as it does today, so that I don't lose that information just because the layout changed.
12. As a player, I want a Housekeeping Staffer working a Job to visibly travel to and stay at the actual Room they're cleaning, so that "who's cleaning what" is readable from the building view itself.
13. As a player, I want a Kitchen Staffer working a Job to visibly travel to and stay at the actual breakfast/dinner queue entry they're serving, so that Terrace staffing is just as legible as Housekeeping.
14. As a player, I want the Terrace to be its own fixed floor in the building, above Reception and below the growable Room floors, so that it reads as a permanent fixture rather than a menu entry.
15. As a player, I want the Terrace's breakfast and dinner queues to render as actors standing on the Terrace floor, so that waiting Diners are as visible as the Reception queue.
16. As a player, I want to drag a Staffer onto a specific waiting Diner to Stack a second cook onto serving them, so that Terrace Stacking works the same direct way Room-cleaning Stacking does.
17. As a player, I want tapping the Terrace structure to still open its existing Kitchen-staffing/Daily-Special/Upgrades modal, so that nothing about Terrace administration regresses.
18. As a player, I want Room floors ordered bottom-to-top by ascending unlock star (cheaper/earlier Room types low, fancier/later ones high), so that the building visibly grows more impressive as I progress.
19. As a player, I want each Room floor to still show a Build Slot at the end of its row when under its instance cap, so that building another instance stays a single tap/drop, exactly as today.
20. As a player, I want tapping a built Room (with no guest selected) to still open its Upgrade menu, so that the upgrade flow is unaffected by the reskin.
21. As a player, I want the whole building to scroll vertically once it's taller than the screen, with every floor keeping the same height, so that drag targets stay just as easy to hit no matter how large my hotel gets.
22. As a player, I want the always-visible stats strip (Cash/Hearts/Reputation/Stars/Day/Season/Pause/Play/Fast) to stay pinned at the very top, unaffected by everything else changing below it, so that I never lose sight of my hotel's vitals.
23. As a player, I want tapping the Reception structure to open one modal with Prices, Hire, Reports, and Reviews as tabs/sections, so that I still have one clear place for hotel-wide administration once the bottom menu bar is gone.
24. As a player, I want adjusting a Room type's price multiplier to still work exactly as it does today, just reached from the new Reception modal instead of a bottom-bar button.
25. As a player, I want to see day-to-day events (arrivals, reviews, turned-away guests, forecasts) as brief messages that appear near where they happened, so that I still get the ambient feedback the day log used to give without a dedicated ticker taking up screen space.
26. As a player, I want a checkout's review message to appear near the Room it came from, so that I can connect the feedback to the stay that earned it.
27. As a staffed-Bellhop hotel owner, I want check-in to become a visible Escort — the Bellhop walks the new guest up to their Room over a delay based on the Bellhop's Skill — instead of the guest just appearing there instantly, so that staffing my Bellhop Station feels like it does something I can watch.
28. As a player, I want a higher-Skill Bellhop to complete an Escort faster than a lower-Skill one, so that Bellhop Skill matters the same way Housekeeping Skill already does for cleaning speed.
29. As a player, I want multiple Staffers assigned to Bellhop to each be able to escort a different Party at the same time, so that double-staffing Bellhop has a real parallel-throughput payoff, mirroring how double-staffing Housekeeping already does.
30. As a player, I want a Party seated while every assigned Bellhop is already busy escorting someone else to simply wait for the next free Bellhop rather than being treated as if Bellhop were unstaffed, so that a busy-but-staffed Bellhop Station isn't penalized the same as an empty one.
31. As a player, I want an unstaffed Bellhop Station to keep behaving exactly as it does today (a flat delay, no visible Escort), so that the existing graceful-degradation behavior for going understaffed isn't disrupted by this change.
32. As a developer, I want the Escort's concurrency/Skill-scaled-delay logic added to `SimController`'s existing per-Staffer Job pattern (mirroring `_tick_housekeeping()`), so that Bellhop's implementation doesn't invent a second Job-tracking shape alongside Housekeeping's and Kitchen's.
33. As a developer, I want every existing `Sim`/`GameState` public call (`seat_party`, `assign_staffer`, `stack_staffer_on_room`, `stack_staffer_on_breakfast`, `stack_staffer_on_dinner`, `match_hint`, etc.) to keep its current signature and behavior, so that this reskin doesn't ripple into the sim layer beyond the one deliberate Bellhop Escort addition.
34. As a developer, I want every existing bespoke modal (seat-confirm, build-confirm, stay-info, upgrade, staffer-detail, the Terrace modal) to keep working unchanged, just opened from the new spatial objects instead of button rows, so that ADR-0011's popup work isn't redone.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Unified spatial view**: `ui/lobby_view.gd`, `ui/reception_panel.gd`, `ui/station_panel.gd`, `ui/hotel_panel.gd`, and `ui/terrace_panel.gd` are replaced by one new spatial container (a vertically-scrolling `Control`/`ScrollContainer` tree) that `ui/main_screen.gd` mounts below the unchanged stats strip. The bottom menu bar (`_build_menu_bar()` and its four buttons) and the day-log `RichTextLabel`/`_build_day_log()` are removed from `main_screen.gd` entirely.
- **Floor stack, bottom to top**: Reception (fixed) → Terrace (fixed) → one Floor per unlocked Room type (`GameState.can_build_room_type()`), ordered by ascending `data/rooms.json` `unlock.star` — replacing `hotel_panel.gd`'s current alphabetical `room_type_ids.sort()`. Each Room floor keeps today's per-type row of built-instance cells (`GameState.floor_instance_count()`/`room_instance()`) plus a trailing Build Slot cell under `GameState.can_build_more()`'s cap — same `RoomCellButton` drop-target semantics (Party-seat, Staffer-stack), re-themed as a room rather than a bare button.
- **Actors, not cards**: guests and Staffers render as draggable actor nodes (plain colored shapes, per ADR-0015's staged approach — no new art in this spec) rather than `PartyCardButton`/`StafferCardButton` text buttons. Drag payload shapes (`{"type": "party", "party_id": ...}`, `{"type": "staffer", "staffer_id": ...}`) and drop-target validation (`Sim.match_hint()`, `Sim.can_stack_staffer_on_room()`, etc.) are unchanged — only the visual source/target nodes change. Tap-select-then-tap-target keeps working alongside drag, per ADR-0001/0009.
- **Reception ground floor**: hosts the arrival-queue actors (`Sim.pending_arrivals`), the Reception/Bellhop/Housekeeping Station drop-zones (today's `station_panel.gd` targets), and a new Staff Pool area listing every Staffer with no current Station assignment as an idle draggable actor. Tapping the Reception structure itself (not an actor) opens a new tabbed modal bundling `ui/prices_menu.gd`, `ui/hire_menu.gd`, `ui/reports_menu.gd`, and `ui/reviews_menu.gd`'s existing content unchanged — only their entry point moves.
- **Terrace floor**: fixed, directly above Reception, below the growable Room floors. Hosts the Kitchen Station drop-zone (unchanged from ADR-0009/0010) and renders `Sim.breakfast_queue`/`Sim.walkin_queue` as actors instead of `terrace_panel.gd`'s `QueueEntryButton` list; dragging a Staffer actor onto a specific Diner actor calls the existing `Sim.stack_staffer_on_breakfast()`/`stack_staffer_on_dinner()`. Tapping the Terrace structure keeps opening `ui/terrace_menu.gd` unchanged.
- **Walk-to-target animation**: a new shared animation helper (used by guest seat-in/checkout/turn-away and by Staffer Job-travel) tweens an actor's position from its current spot to a real target node's position, replacing `lobby_view.gd`'s fixed Entrance/Reception/Elevator X-coordinates. A seated guest's actor does not despawn on arrival — it stays parented at the Room cell as that Room's occupant actor for the stay (no idle animation yet, static placeholder) and is despawned only on checkout, when it animates back down to Reception and out.
- **Staff Job travel**: generalizes `lobby_view.gd`'s old decorative, non-spatial bellhop/housekeeper round-trips into real point-to-point moves keyed off `SimController`'s existing per-Staffer Job dicts (`_cleaning_jobs`, `_breakfast_jobs`, `_dinner_jobs`) — a Staffer with an in-flight Job travels to that Job's real Room/queue-entry position and stays until it resolves, then returns to their Station.
- **Toasts replace the day log**: `main_screen.gd`'s `_on_day_summary`/`_on_review_posted`/`_on_forecast_ready` handlers drive brief, auto-dismissing toast nodes positioned near the relevant spatial anchor (Reception for arrivals/turn-aways/forecasts, the specific Room cell for a checkout's review) instead of appending to a ticker. Toast content is the same text these handlers already format.
- **Bellhop Escort (ADR-0017, implements ADR-0014)**: `SimController._start_checkin()`'s staffed branch (currently instant) is replaced with a per-Bellhop-Staffer Job model mirroring `_tick_housekeeping()`: each assigned, currently-idle Bellhop Staffer claims the next Party awaiting Escort and works it down over a new `data/balance.json` `stations.bellhop.escort_ticks_by_skill` table (shaped like `stations.housekeeping.clean_ticks_by_skill`), after which the Room occupies and the guest actor's walk-to-Room animation plays alongside the Bellhop's own actor. The unstaffed branch (flat `unstaffed_checkin_delay_ticks` delay) is unchanged. A Party seated while every assigned Bellhop is already mid-Escort joins a new waiting-for-Bellhop queue rather than falling back to the unstaffed flat delay; it's picked up by the next Bellhop that frees up.
- **Modules touched**: `ui/main_screen.gd` (mounts the new spatial view, drops the menu bar/day log), a new spatial-view module (name TBD by the implementing agent — e.g. `ui/hotel_view.gd`) replacing `lobby_view.gd`/`reception_panel.gd`/`station_panel.gd`/`hotel_panel.gd`/`terrace_panel.gd`, and `autoload/sim_controller.gd` (`_start_checkin()` and its `_tick_checkins()` companion) plus `data/balance.json` for the Escort mechanic. `GameState`/`Sim`'s existing public API surface is otherwise unchanged.

## Testing Decisions

- Good tests here assert observable `GameState`/`SimController` outcomes and emitted `EventBus` signals — never internal function call counts, private helper behavior, or anything about node layout/animation timing.
- **Bellhop Escort (new Sim-layer behavior)** is the one piece of this spec with real logic to test headlessly, via `SimController`'s public API, mirroring `tests/test_roster_station.gd`'s and `tests/test_stacking.gd`'s conventions: a staffed Escort taking measurably longer at Skill 1 than Skill 5; two assigned Bellhops each completing a different Escort in parallel; a third Party seated while both assigned Bellhops are busy landing in the waiting-for-Bellhop queue and getting picked up once one frees; the unstaffed flat-delay path staying byte-for-byte the same as today; and a full multi-day batch run (`sim/batch_runner.gd`) completing with no Party stuck in an unresolvable Escort-wait state.
- **The spatial view/actors/animations/toasts** (everything else in this spec) get no automated test coverage, matching this project's existing convention — no `ui/*.gd` file has ever had a GUT test, and the sim/presentation split is exactly what makes that safe (the UI is a thin layer over the tested `GameState`/`SimController` API). Verification is manual: run the game and drive the golden path (guest arrives → drag-seat → walk-in animation → Escort with/without a Bellhop → stay → checkout) plus the Staffer paths (drag-assign, Stacking, Job travel) and the Reception admin modal, per each ticket's checklist below.

## Out of Scope

- Any new guest/Staffer art or idle animation beyond a static placeholder shape — ADR-0015's staged approach (Manny is the only real animation) is unchanged; richer per-actor animation is explicit future follow-up.
- Training (ADR-0012) and Traits (ADR-0013) — both remain unimplemented, untouched by this spec.
- Any change to seating math, pricing, Patience, Satisfaction, Reviews, Reputation, or Hearts beyond the Bellhop Escort delay itself — those formulas are unchanged.
- Rebalancing any existing `data/balance.json` numbers beyond adding the new `stations.bellhop.escort_ticks_by_skill` table — its values should be illustrative/wired-correctly (roughly mirroring Housekeeping's curve), not tuned; tuning is a follow-up batch-runner exercise.
- Rewriting `GUIDE.md` — this spec changes behavior GUIDE.md may describe, but updating player-facing docs is a separate follow-up.
- A `/domain-modeling` pass to add any new glossary terms this spec introduces (e.g. Staff Pool, Escort queue) to `CONTEXT.md` — the two new ADRs (0016, 0017) capture the decisions; glossary cleanup is left for that skill.
