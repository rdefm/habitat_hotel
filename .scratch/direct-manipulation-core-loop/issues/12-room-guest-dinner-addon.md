# 12 — Room-guest dinner add-on

**What to build:** At check-in (seating), a Party can opt into a dinner add-on for their stay. An occupied Room whose guest opted in shows that on its Room card, so the player can tell at a glance which occupied Rooms also expect Kitchen service that evening. The add-on feeds the same dinner queue and scoring as a Walk-in Diner.
