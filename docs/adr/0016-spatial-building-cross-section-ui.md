# Spatial building cross-section replaces the panel/list UI

The current UI amends ADR-0001/0009's direct-manipulation model correctly but renders it as stacked text-button lists (`ui/reception_panel.gd`, `ui/station_panel.gd`, `ui/hotel_panel.gd`, `ui/terrace_panel.gd`) plus a purely decorative animation strip (`ui/lobby_view.gd`) that never reflects real drag/seating targets, a bottom menu bar (Prices/Hire/Reports/Reviews), and a day-log ticker. We're replacing all of it with one unified, vertically-scrolling spatial view: a building cross-section with Reception as a fixed ground floor, the Terrace as its own fixed floor above that, and Room-type Floors stacked above those in ascending unlock-star order (correcting the current alphabetical `room_type_id` sort). Guests and Staffers render as actors — draggable, and persistent once placed (a seated guest's actor stays as the room's idle occupant for their whole stay; an assigned Staffer's actor stays near their Station) — rather than button cards, with walk-in/checkout/turn-away and Job-work (Housekeeping/Kitchen) all animating to their real target position instead of `lobby_view.gd`'s abstracted "elevator" stand-in. Unassigned Staffers idle in a dedicated Staff Pool area at ground level. The bottom menu bar is retired: Prices/Hire/Reports/Reviews move behind tapping the Reception structure as one tabbed modal, mirroring how ADR-0010 already bundles Kitchen staffing/Daily Special/Upgrades behind tapping the Terrace. The day log ticker is retired in favor of transient toast notifications anchored near the relevant spatial position.

This is a rendering-layer amendment only: ADR-0001's no-auto-matcher principle, ADR-0009's tap-and-drag coexisting gestures, and every existing `Sim`/`GameState` call they drive are unchanged. ADR-0011's bespoke modals (seat-confirm, stay-info, build-confirm, staffer-detail, the Terrace modal) are unchanged, just opened by tapping the new spatial objects instead of button rows.

Status: superseded by ADR-0020 (the Node2D world it introduced replaces this Control-tree "spatial building cross-section" outright; ticket 17 deleted the whole view — `hotel_view.gd`, `hotel_panel.gd`, `reception_panel.gd`, `station_panel.gd`, `terrace_panel.gd`, `staffer_card.gd`, `station_card.gd`, `room_occupancy_layer.gd`, `staff_job_travel_layer.gd` — and the development toggle that let both run side by side) (was: accepted, amends the rendering of ADR-0001/0009/0010; supersedes `lobby_view.gd`'s decorative-only role from ADR-0015's framing — Manny/placeholder staging was unaffected, actors were still plain shapes except Manny).
