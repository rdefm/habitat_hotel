# Staff have a Skill per Station, freely reassignable — not one fixed Role

The plan gave each Staffer one permanent Role (Receptionist, Bellhop, Housekeeper, Chef) fixed at hire/generation. The prototype instead gives every Staffer a Skill rating (1–5) at all four Stations, lopsided toward a home specialty, and lets the player reassign anyone to any Station at any time — reassignment interrupts whatever job that Staffer was mid-way through (a half-cleaned Room reverts to dirty, an interrupted dinner/breakfast job just goes back to waiting).

We're keeping the Station model: free reassignment is itself a strategic lever (cover a lull, specialize for a rush) rather than a one-time hiring decision. This is a bigger data-model commitment than it looks — every Staffer needs a tuned skill profile across all four Stations instead of a single Role-appropriate stat block, and balancing must account for players stacking specialists at will rather than a fixed role distribution.

Status: accepted, supersedes the Roles model in `hotel_habitat_plan.md` §3.5 and GUIDE.md §13.
