# Staff Roster grows by unlocking named individuals, not a hire/fire pool

The plan called for 3 authored starters plus a procedurally generated weekly hire pool that scales with star level, with firing available at any time. The prototype instead ships a small fixed cast of named, authored individuals (Biscuit, Marlon, Shelly) with no hiring or firing at all.

We're going with the prototype's direction, generalized: the Roster grows by unlocking more authored, named Staffers over time (star level, cash milestones, or story beats), not by generating and firing anonymous candidates. This keeps every Staffer a character worth knowing rather than disposable inventory, at the cost of needing more authored content per Staffer (name, personality, stat profile) instead of procedural generation doing that work.
